Service status

NEARVOLT EV is currently in its pre-launch demonstration phase. Charging and booking services are not yet available. App screens, prices, hosts and locations shown on this website are fictional demonstrations. Charging-time figures are estimates for planning only. No partnerships, approvals or host listings are claimed.

Privacy

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers and sends no personal data anywhere. It stores two small values in your browser’s session storage — whether the intro has played in this session and your intro sound choice — which are deleted when you close the tab. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request data (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement.

Safety

Information on this site is general. Always follow your vehicle manufacturer’s charging instructions and use only correctly installed, earthed and protected equipment.
