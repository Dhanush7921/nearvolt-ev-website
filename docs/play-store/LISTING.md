Running low on charge? NEARVOLT EV connects electric car, scooter and bike drivers with nearby verified home charging hosts in Visakhapatnam.
• See verified hosts near you with straight-line distance, power and connectors
• Request emergency charging and get a response within minutes
• Get directions with Google Maps once a host accepts
• Your safety first: every host is manually verified, including an electrical safety check, before appearing
• Hosts: list your charging point, set your hours, and accept requests on your terms
NEARVOLT EV connects drivers with independent hosts. We do not manufacture, install or supply charging equipment. In an emergency involving danger to people, call 112.
