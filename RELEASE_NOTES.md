# Release notes

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1) — not yet released
First production version of NEARVOLT EV for Visakhapatnam.

**Drivers:** sign in with Google, email link or mobile OTP · set vehicle and connector · find verified home charging hosts near your location (or a location you enter) with straight-line distance and live bookable status · request emergency charging with compatibility confirmation · see the host's address and navigate with Google Maps once accepted · confirm arrival and completion · rate the visit · history, notifications, support with urgent unsafe-charging reports, account deletion.

**Hosts:** register a charging point with equipment, connector, power and hours · upload photos and safety documents · submit for manual verification (electrical safety check required) · go online/offline or online for a set time · accept or decline requests · confirm arrival, start and complete sessions · session history and feedback.

**Platform:** secure admin dashboard (host verification checklist, suspensions, complaints, audit log, maintenance mode, minimum app version, feature flags, service areas, Sheets sync status, CSV export) · Google Sheets reporting mirror · push notifications without sensitive lock-screen content.

Not included (planned): online payments (Razorpay), custom domain, iOS app, Telugu/Hindi translations.

Play Store "What's new" text: "First release: find verified home EV charging nearby in Visakhapatnam, request emergency charging, and navigate with Google Maps. Hosts can register and manage requests."
