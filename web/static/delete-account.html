Delete your NEARVOLT EV account

Fastest: open the NEARVOLT EV app → Account → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately.

If you no longer have the app, send a request below. We will verify that you own the account (using the email or mobile number you signed in with) before deleting it.

What is deleted

Your profile, vehicle details, notifications, device tokens and sign-in account. For hosts, your listing details, address, photos and documents are removed.

What is kept

Charging-request records without your profile details, where needed for safety investigations, disputes and legal obligations. See the Privacy Policy.
