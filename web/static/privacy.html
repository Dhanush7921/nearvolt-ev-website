NEARVOLT EV

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0. This policy describes how the NEARVOLT EV app and website handle personal data. It is pending review by qualified legal counsel before public launch.

Who we are

NEARVOLT EV is an emergency EV charging assistance platform that connects electric-vehicle drivers with nearby, verified charging hosts, starting in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. We do not manufacture, install or supply charging equipment. Hosts are independent.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, and the email address and/or mobile number you sign in with. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). We never receive or store your Google password or one-time codes.
  • Vehicle: vehicle type, connector type and (optionally) model, used to show compatible hosts.
  • Location (drivers): your device location, or a location you type, only when you search or create a request. We do not track location in the background.
  • Hosts: listing name, private address and coordinates, charging equipment details, opening hours, photos of the charging point and electrical safety documents for verification.
  • Requests: charging requests, their status history, ratings, feedback and support tickets.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can send request updates, and crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics) in release builds.
  • Sign-in records: the time and method of sign-in, for security and account support.

How we use it

To operate the service: match drivers with compatible verified hosts, process charging requests, send notifications, verify host safety, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support and resolve disputes. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Location privacy

A host's exact address and coordinates are never shown in search results. They are shared with a driver only after the host accepts that driver's request, and only while the visit is active. Search results show straight-line distance only.

Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored in Google Cloud and Firebase services (Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Run, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics). Operational records are also kept in a private Google Sheet accessible only to authorised NEARVOLT EV staff. If you sign in with a mobile number, the number is sent to our SMS provider (MSG91) to deliver the one-time code. Host verification documents are private and viewable only by authorised staff through short-lived links.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your profile, notifications and device tokens and your sign-in account. Charging-request records are retained without your profile details where needed for safety investigations, disputes and legal obligations. Security and rate-limit records expire automatically.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time in the app (Account → Delete account) or via this page.
  • Turn off location or notification permissions in your phone settings; you can still enter a location manually.
  • Contact us to access or correct your data.

Children

NEARVOLT EV is intended for adults. Do not use the service if you are under 18.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access is checked on our servers for every request, and staff access requires two-step verification and is audit-logged. No system is completely secure; report concerns through in-app support.

Changes

We will notify users in the app before material changes take effect.

Contact

Use Support in the app. Operator registration and grievance-officer details will be published here before launch.
