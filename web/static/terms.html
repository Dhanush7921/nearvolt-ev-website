The service

NEARVOLT EV is a platform that connects electric-vehicle drivers who need charging with independent charging hosts. NEARVOLT EV does not own, operate, manufacture, install or supply any charging equipment and is not a party to the charging itself.

No guarantee of availability

Host availability shown in the app is provided by hosts and may change at any time. A request is confirmed only when a host accepts it. In an emergency involving danger to people, call 112.

Drivers

  • Confirm that your vehicle and charging equipment are compatible before plugging in.
  • Inspect the socket and cable; stop immediately and report if you notice heat, burning smell, sparks or damage.
  • Respect the host's property and instructions.

Hosts

  • Provide accurate information about your property, permission to host, and electrical equipment.
  • Listings go live only after NEARVOLT EV staff verify them, including an electrical safety inspection. Verification is a platform-eligibility check, not a certification of your installation; you remain responsible for keeping your installation safe and compliant.
  • Change your listing and go offline whenever the charging point is unavailable or unsafe.

Payments

The app does not currently process payments. Any arrangement between a driver and a host is outside the app.

Disputes and safety reports

Report problems through the app. We review safety reports with priority and may suspend a listing while we investigate.
