Our concept

The nearest charger might be a neighbour’s.

Public charging is growing, but it is still uneven. Many homes, shops and offices already have a safe charger or a suitable socket that sits idle for most of the day.

NEARVOLT EV is designed to bring both together in one place: public stations and verified private hosts, filtered for your vehicle, so a short detour can replace a long search.

  • For electric cars, scooters and bikes
  • Public stations and participating private hosts
  • Andhra Pradesh first, with Visakhapatnam proposed as the pilot city, subject to demand validation

For charging hosts

Your charger can serve the neighbourhood.

If you have a home wallbox, a charger at your shop or office, or a properly protected socket for two-wheelers, you could offer it to nearby drivers at the times and price you choose.

Our vision

A charging network that grows street by street.

We want charging to feel as close and ordinary as parking. The plan is to start small and local in Andhra Pradesh, learn from drivers and hosts in a pilot, and expand to other cities only after the pilot meets its milestones.

Safety and trust

Electricity deserves care.

Verified hosts

Hosts are planned to be identity-checked and reviewed before they can be listed.

Proper installations

Chargers and sockets should be on earthed, correctly protected circuits. Extension cords are not a safe substitute.

Honest information

Connector, power, price and access details are shown up front, and drivers can rate every session.

Privacy by default

The app is designed to share only what a session needs. This website collects no personal data.

If a charger, plug or cable is damaged, hot, wet or smells of burning, do not use it. Follow the vehicle manufacturer’s charging instructions at all times.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I use NEARVOLT EV today?

Not yet. NEARVOLT EV is in its pre-launch demonstration phase. There is no live charger map, booking or payment on this website.

Where will it launch?

Andhra Pradesh first. Visakhapatnam is the proposed pilot city, subject to demand validation. Other cities would follow only after the pilot meets its milestones.

How will the app know which chargers suit my vehicle?

You will tell it your vehicle and connector, for example CCS2, Type 2, Type 7 (LECCS) or a portable charger. Options are planned to be filtered by connector and power so incompatible ones stay hidden. See the connector guide.

Does it work for electric scooters and bikes?

Yes, two-wheelers are part of the plan from the start, including suitable sockets for portable chargers and light-EV connectors.

Who can become a host?

Homeowners, shops, offices and other premises with a safe charger or a properly protected socket. Hosts are planned to be verified before listing.

How will pricing work?

Hosts are expected to set their own rate per kWh, per hour or per session, shown to drivers before they travel. Final terms will be published before launch.

Is the car in the film a real product?

No. It is a licensed, logo-free concept-car model rendered in real time for illustration. NEARVOLT EV is a charging platform, not a vehicle maker.
